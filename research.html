Independent Research: Bridge Material vs. Condition

SHPE 2026 STEM Research Competition FHWA National Bridge Inventory, 2025

I studied 11,680 Florida bridges to see if a bridge's material relates to its condition.

  • Analyzed Florida's 2025 FHWA National Bridge Inventory dataset to evaluate the relationship between bridge material type and structural condition.
  • Applied chi-square statistical testing to identify significant differences in poor-condition rates across materials.
  • Steel bridges had the highest poor-condition rate (7.5%), and prestressed concrete had the lowest (1.4%).
  • The steel gap is regional: 22% of steel bridges in Northwest Florida are rated Poor, against 2.5% in the rest of the state.
